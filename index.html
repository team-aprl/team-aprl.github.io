Welcome to APRL DGIST

Autonomy and Perceptual Robotics Lab (APRL)

APRL explores challenges in autonomous navigation and constructing world models for intelligent robots.

aprl.dgist.ac.kr Dept. of Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering DGIST

About APRL

  • Autonomy and Perceptual Robotics Lab (APRL, 로봇인식 및 자율화 연구실) at DGIST has been started in Dec 2024 by Giseop Kim.
  • Our mission is to explore autonomous navigation and robot world models for intelligent robots.

Latest News

All news
  1. May 2026

    Three workshop papers from our lab have been accepted to ICRA 2026. Congrats to Doyeon and Nayak+Hyoseok for their papers accepted to the MM-SpatialAI Workshop, and to Hoyun for his paper accepted to the Workshop on Perceptual Challenges for Planetary Exploration.

  2. Mar 2026

    Our lab has been awarded the NRF Young Researcher Program (Type B) grant from the Ministry of Science and ICT, supporting research on multi-robot autonomy with implicit human-robot interaction (Project title: AIMS) for four years (2026-2029).

  3. Mar 2026

    Welcome new members joining APRL: Doyeon Kim (PhD student), Beomsu Kim (MS student), and Hoyun Kim (MS student).

  4. Feb 2026

    One paper accepted to ICRA 2026. Congratulations to Hyoseok.

  5. Feb 2026

    Giseop has been selected as an early-career researcher for KRoC 2026. He will present his recent work on memory-augmented spatial intelligence for long-term autonomous robot navigation.

  6. Oct 2025

    Jiseon received the Best Poster Award at the HEAI Workshop of IROS 2025.

  7. Sep 2025

    The Robotics and Mechatronics Research Institute of DGIST has been selected for the Ministry of Education's Glocal Lab program. Our lab will conduct research on sensor fusion, robot perception, and cognitive AI for augmented human robots.

  8. Jun 2025

    DGIST has been selected for Korea's InnoCORE initiative on Bio-Embodied Physical AI. Our lab, as part of the DGIST team, is recruiting talented postdocs in SLAM, 3D vision, and vision-language navigation.

  9. May 2025

    Our lab is supported by the AI Star Fellowship from Ministry of Science and ICT for the next six years (2025-2030).
