Welcome to APRL DGIST!

  • Autonomy and Perceptual Robotics Lab (APRL, 로봇인식 및 자율화 연구실) at DGIST has been started in Dec 2024 by Giseop Kim.
  • Our VisionAPRL advances Persistent, Intent-Grounded, and Resilient Embodied Intelligence, enabling robots to retain and update long-term world models, interpret human intent, and maintain reliable autonomy by adapting to disruptions and recovering from failures. To realize this vision, APRL develops spatial intelligence and robot world models that enable robots to navigate, reason, and learn continuously from real-world experience.
